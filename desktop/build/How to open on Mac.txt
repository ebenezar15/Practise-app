HOW TO OPEN CADENZA ON A MAC
============================

1. Drag "Cadenza" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click Cadenza.
   macOS will say it "cannot verify the developer" (Cadenza is not yet registered with Apple).
   Click "Done" (or "Cancel").

3. Open  System Settings  >  Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to the message about Cadenza. Confirm with your password.
   (On older macOS you can instead right-click Cadenza > Open > Open.)

   You only need to do this once.

If macOS still says the app is "damaged", open Terminal and run:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Cadenza.app

then open Cadenza again.

When Cadenza asks to use the microphone, click "Allow" — that is how it hears your instrument.
Works on Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and Intel Macs, macOS 10.15 or newer.
